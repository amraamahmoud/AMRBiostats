Refund & Cancellation Policy

How cancellations, booking transfers, and refunds are handled.

Last updated: 9 September 2026

Courses & training

If you cancel before a live course begins, your payment for that course will be refunded in full. You may choose a transfer to another available course or cohort instead; any price difference will be agreed beforehand.

After a course starts, you may cancel the remaining part. Any refund is based on the amount paid less the value of the part already delivered, using the session or component prices agreed before purchase. We will explain the calculation in writing.

Consultations & rescheduling

Contact us as soon as possible if you need to cancel or reschedule. If you cancel before a consultation begins, your payment for that session will be refunded in full, or you may choose another available appointment.

If you miss an appointment, contact us to discuss rescheduling or cancellation. A missed appointment is not automatically treated as a consultation that was delivered, and this policy does not impose an automatic no-show fee.

Statistical analysis & custom work

If you cancel before agreed project work begins, payment for that work will be refunded in full. After work begins, you may cancel the remaining work and receive a refund for the unperformed portion.

The calculation uses the agreed scope and stage prices, including any agreed preparation or analysis work already performed. Where a stage is only partly completed, we will explain its completed portion and value; we will not charge the full stage merely because it was started.

A completed service is not normally refundable solely because findings are not statistically significant, a hypothesis is unsupported, a journal rejects a manuscript, or you later decide not to use the analysis. This does not limit remedies for genuine errors or work that differs from the agreement. Corrections within the agreed scope are made without an additional fee; new research questions or additional analyses may require a separate quotation.

Recorded courses & digital materials

For recorded content, contact us with the course name and details of your cancellation request or access problem. Any specific access and cancellation conditions will be explained before purchase.

Opening or downloading material does not by itself remove mandatory consumer rights. We will assess the request in light of the content supplied, any service already used, and applicable rights. Report faulty access or content that materially differs from what was promised so that we can arrange an appropriate remedy.

If we cancel or a service has a problem

If AMRBiostats cancels a paid course and you do not accept an alternative, the full course payment will be refunded. If we cancel a consultation before it begins, the full session payment will be refunded unless you choose to reschedule.

If we cannot complete another agreed service, we will refund the unprovided portion. If work contains an error or materially differs from the agreement, contact us for review and an appropriate correction or refund. Requesting a correction does not waive your legal remedies.

Verified duplicate payments or amounts charged above the agreed price will be refunded.

Refund method & processing

Refunds normally return through the original payment method and relevant payment provider. If this is not supported, an alternative method will be agreed with you. We do not require payment-card details by email or message.

We will confirm the refundable amount and initiate the refund promptly, within any applicable legal deadline. We will provide confirmation when it has been submitted. The time taken for a credit to appear depends on the bank or payment provider; this does not extend or remove our legal obligations. Contact us with your reference if an expected refund has not arrived.

Request a cancellation, refund or transfer

Email consultation@amrbiostats.com. Include your name, course or service, payment date, and booking or transaction reference if available.

If something went wrong, briefly describe the issue so we can investigate. No reason is required where an unconditional cancellation right applies. Please do not send card details or identifiable participant data.

AMRBiostats provides services and digital learning rather than physical goods. Exchanges are therefore handled as agreed booking transfers or corrections to access. A transfer is optional and does not replace a refund to which you are entitled.

Consumer rights & related terms

This policy applies alongside our Terms & Conditions.
